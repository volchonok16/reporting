Consent to Personal Data Processing

Edition 2 of 14 October 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The Russian version is the governing one.

By ticking “I give my consent to personal data processing” when signing up for Simmetrika (simmetrika.online), I freely, of my own will and in my own interest consent to the operator — the Simmetrika administration (email no-reply@simmetrika.online) — processing my personal data on the following terms.

1. Data

Email address, name (if I gave one), password as a hash, interface settings, IP address and technical details of requests to the Service.

2. Purposes

Signing up and signing in; keeping my files and giving access to them to people I choose; sending service letters (confirmation codes, invitations, notifications); protecting the Service and accounts; answering my requests.

3. Actions

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, changing), retrieval, use, giving access to people I work with in the Service, blocking, deletion and destruction. Processing is automated.

4. Term and withdrawal

The consent is valid while my account exists or until withdrawn. It can be withdrawn by deleting the account in the Service (the account menu → “Delete account…”) — the data goes at once — or by writing to no-reply@simmetrika.online from the account’s address — then within 30 days, unless the law requires keeping them longer.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.
