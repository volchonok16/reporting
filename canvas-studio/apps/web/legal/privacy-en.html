Privacy Policy

Edition 2 of 14 October 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The Russian version is the governing one.

This policy describes what personal data Simmetrika (simmetrika.online, the “Service”) collects, why, where and for how long. It follows Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data” of 27 July 2006.

In short: we keep only what the Service cannot work without — your email, your files, and a name if you choose to give one. No ads, no visit counters, no third-party analytics, no selling of data.

1. Operator

The personal data operator is the Simmetrika administration. Contact email: no-reply@simmetrika.online.

2. What data we process

  • Account: email address, password (stored only as an irreversible scrypt hash — we never know the password itself), a name if you gave one (optional: without it others see your email), sign-up date, the date and edition of your consent to data processing.
  • Interface settings: language, theme, panel layout — so they are the same on all your devices.
  • Your materials: files, their versions, comments, projects, teams, file links and the list of people with access.
  • Invitees’ email: when you invite someone to a file or team, we keep their address and send them an invitation. By inviting you confirm you may give us that address.
  • Other people’s data in your files: if you put other people’s personal data into files, we process it only on your instruction — keep it and show it to those you gave access — and use it for nothing of our own. You provide the legal grounds for it (see 3.6 of the Terms of Use).
  • Technical data: IP address and request details (time, page address, browser) in web server logs; the IP address is also used to protect against password guessing.

If you work without an account, files stay in your browser and never reach us.

3. Why

  • to create an account, confirm the address and let you in;
  • to keep your files and show them to people you gave access;
  • to send letters the Service needs: confirmation and password codes, invitations, comment notifications (these can be turned off in settings);
  • to protect the Service and accounts from break-ins and abuse;
  • to answer your requests.

We send no marketing mail.

4. Legal basis

Your consent given at sign-up (consent text) and performance of the Terms of Use you are a party to.

5. Where data is kept and who gets it

5.1. Data is kept on a server rented from the hosting provider Beget LLC in the Russian Federation. Letters are sent through the same provider’s mail server.

5.2. Other people see your data only inside the Service: your name and email are visible to people you work with on shared files and in teams.

5.3. We do not sell or pass data to third parties except where the law directly requires it. There is no cross-border transfer.

5.4. Google Fonts are downloaded by our server, not by your browser, so your IP address is not sent to Google.

6. How long

Account data and files are kept while the account exists. An account deleted in the Service goes at once; on a request by email or withdrawal of consent — within 30 days, unless the law requires keeping them longer. Web server logs are deleted automatically as they rotate.

7. Cookies and browser storage

The Service uses one technical cookie — the sign-in session (valid for 30 days, not readable by page scripts). Signing in is impossible without it, so it needs no separate consent. Settings and files made without an account are kept in your browser’s storage and not sent to us. There are no counters or advertising cookies.

8. Protection

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS); passwords, sessions and emailed codes are stored only as hashes. Sign-in and confirmation attempts are limited. Only the Service administration has access to the server. In case of a leak we will notify Roskomnadzor within the time the law sets (the first notice within 24 hours) and inform affected users.

9. Your rights

You can learn what data about you we keep, correct it, have it deleted, withdraw consent, and appeal our actions to Roskomnadzor or a court. You can delete the account yourself: the account menu → “Delete account…”. For anything else write to no-reply@simmetrika.online from the account’s address — we reply within 10 working days. Withdrawing consent means deleting the account: the Service cannot work without an email.

10. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 14.

11. Changes

A new edition is published on this page with its date. If a change affects what data we collect or who gets it, we will warn you in advance and ask for new consent where needed.
